## English

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Ralli is a tennis practice app made by [COMPANY NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Ralli.

### The short version
- Ralli has no accounts and no sign-in.
- Your lesson notes, practice history, results and preferences are stored **only on your iPhone**. We cannot see them.
- We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.
- We do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

### Information stored on your device
- Lesson notes and their categories, and practice sessions: date, drill, duration, optional result and reflection.
- Practice preferences: focus, time, solo/partner, level.
- App settings: favourites, spoken cues on/off, unfinished note and session drafts.

This information stays on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted too. Device backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you turned it on) may include it, under Apple's terms.

### Microphone and speech recognition
When you tap **Dictate**, Ralli asks for microphone and speech recognition permission and turns your speech into text for your note.
- On iPhones that support it, speech is recognised **entirely on the device**.
- On other iPhones, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio. Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.
- Ralli does not save audio recordings. Only the text you keep in your note is stored on your device.

You can use Ralli without the microphone and change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

### Notifications
Ralli may ask to send notifications so it can tell you when a practice step ends while the app is in the background. These notifications are created on your device; we do not send them from a server.

### Diagnostics
If you allow your iPhone to *Share With App Developers* (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us. These reports do not contain your notes and are used only to fix problems.

### Links to other websites
Some drills link to official coaching guides on third-party websites (for example the LTA). Those websites have their own privacy policies.

### Children
Ralli is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

### Your rights
Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: you can edit or delete notes and sessions in the app, or delete the app. If you have questions, or want to make a request under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK, contact us at [E-MAIL].

### Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

**Contact:** [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS], [E-MAIL]
