## English (U.S.)
- **Name (22/30):** Ralli: Tennis Practice
- **Subtitle (27/30):** Your lesson, taken to court
- **Keywords (96/100):** `tennis,drills,practice,serve,forehand,backhand,volley,lesson,notes,training,court,timer,footwork`
- **Promotional text:** Save the tip your coach gave you, practise it with a clear session, and see what changes. New: 3D stroke animations with slow motion.
- **Description:**

  Ralli turns your tennis lesson into practice that sticks.

  SAVE THE TIP THAT MATTERS
  Say it out loud after your lesson. Ralli turns it into a note and brings it back before your next session.

  TAKE IT TO COURT
  Pick a short session that fits your time, level and setup: solo, with a partner or against a wall. Clear steps, a big timer you can read in the sun, and a sound and vibration when each step ends, so your phone can stay in your bag.

  SEE HOW IT'S DONE
  3D animations show the order and rhythm of each stroke (forehand, two-handed backhand, serve, volley, overhead and split step) from three camera angles, with slow motion.

  SEE WHAT CHANGES
  Track the days you practise and repeat simple checks. No fake scores and no guilt about missed days.

  PRIVATE BY DESIGN
  No account. Your notes stay on your iPhone.

  Ralli does not replace a coach or medical advice. Warm up and stop if you feel pain.

## İnceleme notu (App Review'a)
> Ralli does not require an account. To see the main flow: open the app, tap "Set it up for me", save preferences, start the recommended session, then open "See the motion and setup" to view the 3D animation. The microphone is used only when the user taps "Dictate" in a lesson note.
